| `test-stubs-drift-from-the-signatures-they-stand-in-for.md` | active | - | A stand-in installed over a real callable is written against the signature of the day, and nothing connects the two afterwards: a delegating stub stops measuring what it was installed to measure and raises `TypeError` about the argument shape instead, and a raising stub meant to prove a path is never reached reports that same `TypeError` rather than its own cause.
